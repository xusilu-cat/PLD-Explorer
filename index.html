把你的瞬变事件序列，放进随机性–混沌相空间

本工具复现文章 “Fast radio bursts, magnetars and earthquakes: Their ‘family feud’?” 中的 Pincus–Lyapunov 图（PLD）。上传一组爆发的时间与能量序列， 即可计算 Pincus Index（PI，随机性） 与 Lyapunov Exponent（LE，混沌性）， 并与重复快速射电暴、磁星耀发、脉冲星 glitch、地震和太阳耀发放在同一张图上比较。

This tool reproduces the Pincus–Lyapunov diagram (PLD) of “Fast radio bursts, magnetars and earthquakes: Their ‘family feud’?”. Upload a time–energy sequence of bursts and it computes the Pincus index (PI, stochasticity) and the Lyapunov exponent (LE, chaoticity), then places your data on the same diagram as repeating FRBs, magnetar bursts, pulsar glitches, earthquakes and solar flares.

时间与能量的换算

  • 等待时间 Δt 由相邻事件时间差得到；MJD 会乘以 86400 转成秒。
  • 能量差分 ΔE 由相邻事件能量差得到；能量的绝对单位、距离、辐射口径都不影响结果，因为两条序列都会做 min–max 归一化。
  • 如果你的原始时间是 秒（例如任务经过时 MET），上传时把“新数据的时间单位”切换为“秒”即可；由于两条序列都会归一化，线性时标不影响 PI / LE。若有条件，仍建议换算成 MJD 以便与文章数据直接对照。

注意事项

  1. 时间顺序：无需预先排序，工具会按时间升序排列后再计算。
  2. 时间精度：MJD 请保留足够小数位（建议 ≥ 5 位）。爆发等待时间往往只有几十到几百秒，而 MJD 的 1 天 = 86400 s，时间被截断到 0.001 天就等于 86 s 的误差，会显著污染等待时间序列。
  3. 样本量：差分后至少需要 ~30 个点才能得到稳定的 PI 与 LE；少于 8 个点会被拒绝。样本越短，随机重排带来的涨落越大。
  4. 观测段：默认只取“最活跃”的一段（文章做法），以避免把不同观测日的长空档当成等待时间。若你的数据是连续监测，可在参数里改为“全部事件合并”。
  5. 多文件：可同时上传多个文件，每个文件作为一个独立源，各自分配颜色与标记。
  6. 缺失值 / 非数值行：空行、注释行（# 或 % 开头）与无法解析的行会自动跳过。
  7. 分隔符：逗号、分号、制表符、空格均可自动识别。
  8. 能量为零或负值：允许（差分序列可正可负），只要同一序列内单位一致即可。
为什么统一用 MJD？
MJD 是天文学通用的连续时间标度，避免了日期字符串与时区带来的歧义。文章中的 FRB、磁星、脉冲星与地震目录均以 MJD 记录； 少数 X 射线样本原始文件用的是任务经过时（秒）——由于 PI / LE 只作用在归一化后的序列上，线性尺度变换不改变结果， 这些样本在内置数据中仍保留秒单位并已在界面上标注。

From time and energy to the two sequences

  • The waiting-time series Δt comes from consecutive time differences; MJD is multiplied by 86400 to convert days into seconds.
  • The energy-increment series ΔE comes from consecutive energy differences. The absolute energy unit, distance and beaming assumptions do not matter, because both series are min–max normalised.
  • If your original times are in seconds (e.g. mission elapsed time), switch “Time unit of new data” to seconds. Because both series are normalised, a linear rescaling of the time axis does not change PI or LE — still, converting to MJD is recommended so results can be compared directly with the published data.

Practical notes

  1. Ordering: no need to sort — the tool sorts by time before computing.
  2. Time precision: keep enough decimals in MJD (≥ 5 recommended). Waiting times between bursts are often only tens to hundreds of seconds, while 1 MJD day = 86400 s; truncating to 0.001 d introduces an 86 s error and badly corrupts the waiting-time series.
  3. Sample size: after differencing you need at least ~30 points for a stable PI and LE; fewer than 8 points is rejected. Shorter samples fluctuate more because of the random shuffles.
  4. Observing sessions: by default only the most active session is used (as in the paper), so that long gaps between observing days are not mistaken for waiting times. Switch to “all events” for continuous monitoring data.
  5. Multiple files: several files can be uploaded at once; each becomes an independent source with its own colour and marker.
  6. Missing / non-numeric rows: blank lines, comment lines (starting with # or %) and unparsable rows are skipped.
  7. Delimiters: comma, semicolon, tab and whitespace are all detected automatically.
  8. Zero or negative energies: allowed (the increment series may change sign) as long as the unit is consistent within a series.
Why MJD everywhere?
MJD is the standard continuous time scale in astronomy and avoids the ambiguity of date strings and time zones. The FRB, magnetar, pulsar and earthquake catalogues in the paper are all recorded in MJD. A few X-ray samples are stored as mission elapsed time in seconds; because PI and LE act on normalised series, a linear rescaling does not change the result, so those samples keep their second-based time stamps and are flagged in the interface.

数学原理

Pincus Index 和 Lyapunov Exponent 这两个统计量描述了两种“不可预测性”： Pincus Index 量化序列中重复模式的缺失（随机性）， Lyapunov Exponent 量化系统对初始条件的敏感程度（混沌性）。

The two statistics, the Pincus Index and the Lyapunov Exponent, describe two kinds of unpredictability: the Pincus Index quantifies the absence of repeating patterns in a series (randomness), while the Lyapunov Exponent quantifies how sensitive the system is to its initial conditions (chaos).

Pincus Index 随机性指标

① 嵌入向量与相似判定

对序列 X = {x1, …, xN}，取嵌入维数 m，第 i 个嵌入向量由连续的 m 个样本构成：

[xi, xi+1, …, xi+m−1]

两个向量的距离取切比雪夫距离 d = maxk |xi+k − xj+k|，若 d < r 则判定“相似”。

② 近似熵 ApEn

ApEn(m, r, N) = − 1N − m ∑ N−mi=1 ln( Cm+1i(r)Cmi(r) )

Cmi(r) 是 m 维嵌入空间中与第 i 个向量距离小于 r 的向量占比。ApEn 越小 ⇒ 模式越重复、越可预测。

③ 消除阈值依赖：MaxApEn

MaxApEn = maxr | ApEn(m+1, r) − ApEn(m, r) |

文章在 r ∈ [10−10, 101] 上取 50 个对数等间距阈值，用其中的最大变化量表征该序列的熵水平，从而摆脱单一 r 的选择困难。

④ 与随机基线比较：PI

PI = MaxApEnoriginalMaxApEnshuffled （shuffled：100 次随机重排后取中位数）

PI → 1 表示原始序列已经和随机重排一样无序；PI 越小表示规则性、可预测性越强。文章中时间序列与能量序列各算一次并取平均，误差由两次重排的标准差传播得到（± 后即 1σ 误差）：

PI = PIt + PIE2 ± √¼ (σt2 + σE2)

  • A. Delgado-Bonal, Sci. Rep. 9, 12761 (2019). DOI ↗

Lyapunov Exponent 混沌指标

① 定义

相空间中两条初始相距 |δ0| 的轨道，其分离量随时间近似指数增长：

|δ(t)| ≃ |δ0| eλt

λ > 0 ⇒ 混沌（对初值敏感）；λ < 0 ⇒ 稳定 / 周期。

② 从单变量序列重建相空间

用延迟嵌入把标量序列还原成轨道：xi = [xi, xi+τ, …, xi+(m−1)τ]。文章采用 m = 10（嵌入维数）、矩阵维数 4、τ = 1。

③ Eckmann 等（1986）算法

  1. 对每个轨道点 xi，用切比雪夫距离找到至少 8 个最近邻；
  2. 用最小二乘求局部雅可比矩阵 Ti，使 Ti(xj − xi) ≈ xj+s − xi+s；
  3. 依次对 Ti Qi−1 做 QR 分解，累积 ln |Rkk|；
  4. 对轨道长度平均，并除以步长 τ 与间隔 s，得到完整的 Lyapunov 谱。

取谱中最大者为最大 Lyapunov 指数（MLE）。同样对时间与能量两条序列分别计算后取平均：

LE = λt + λE2

④ 序列的构造

Δti = (ti+1 − ti) × 86400 s, ΔEi = Ei+1 − Ei

两条序列各自 min–max 归一化到 [0, 1] 后再送入上述算法，因此时间单位与能量单位的线性缩放不影响结果。

⑤ 参考文献

  • C. Schölzel, Nonlinear measures for dynamical systems, Zenodo (2019). DOI ↗
  • J. P. Eckmann, S. Oliffson Kamphorst, D. Ruelle, and S. Ciliberto, Phys. Rev. A 34, 4971 (1986). DOI ↗

Pincus Index a measure of stochasticity

① Embedding vectors and the similarity test

For a series X = {x1, …, xN} and embedding dimension m, the i-th embedding vector consists of m consecutive samples:

[xi, xi+1, …, xi+m−1]

The distance between two vectors is the Chebyshev distance d = maxk |xi+k − xj+k|; two vectors are “similar” when d < r.

② Approximate entropy, ApEn

ApEn(m, r, N) = − 1N − m ∑ N−mi=1 ln( Cm+1i(r)Cmi(r) )

Cmi(r) is the fraction of vectors in the m-dimensional embedding space lying within r of the i-th vector. Smaller ApEn ⇒ more repetitive, more predictable.

③ Removing the tolerance dependence: MaxApEn

MaxApEn = maxr | ApEn(m+1, r) − ApEn(m, r) |

The paper scans 50 logarithmically spaced tolerances over r ∈ [10−10, 101] and uses the largest change as the entropy level of the series, avoiding the need to pick a single r.

④ Comparison with a random baseline: PI

PI = MaxApEnoriginalMaxApEnshuffled （shuffled: median over 100 random permutations）

PI → 1 means the original series is already as irregular as a random shuffle; smaller PI means more regularity and predictability. The paper computes it once for the time series and once for the energy series, then averages the two; the error is propagated from their shuffle standard deviations (± gives the 1σ error):

PI = PIt + PIE2 ± √¼ (σt2 + σE2)

Lyapunov Exponent a measure of chaos

① Definition

Two orbits initially separated by |δ0| in phase space separate approximately exponentially:

|δ(t)| ≃ |δ0| eλt

λ > 0 ⇒ chaotic (sensitive to initial conditions); λ < 0 ⇒ stable / periodic.

② Reconstructing phase space from a scalar series

Delay embedding reconstructs the orbit: xi = [xi, xi+τ, …, xi+(m−1)τ]. The paper uses m = 10 (embedding dimension), matrix dimension 4 and τ = 1.

③ The Eckmann et al. (1986) algorithm

  1. For each orbit point xi, find at least 8 nearest neighbours using the Chebyshev distance;
  2. Fit the local Jacobian Ti by least squares so that Ti(xj − xi) ≈ xj+s − xi+s;
  3. QR-decompose Ti Qi−1 successively and accumulate ln |Rkk|;
  4. Average along the orbit and divide by the step τ and the spacing s to obtain the full Lyapunov spectrum.

The largest entry of the spectrum is the maximum Lyapunov exponent (MLE). The same averaging over the time and energy series is applied:

LE = λt + λE2

④ Constructing the series

Δti = (ti+1 − ti) × 86400 s, ΔEi = Ei+1 − Ei

Both series are min–max normalised to [0, 1] before being fed to the algorithms above, so a linear rescaling of the time or energy unit does not affect the result.
